Age suitability

Currawong is rated 13+ on the App Store. It carries live voice between radio amateurs, and that conversation is not moderated.

What the app does

Currawong connects to AllStarLink, EchoLink and M17, voice networks run by the amateur radio community. Once connected, you hear whoever is talking on that node or reflector, and you can talk back. Many of these destinations are also linked to radio transmitters, so what is said can go out over the air.

Why 13+

  • You talk with people you do not know. Anyone with access to a network can speak on it, and conversations are live.
  • Nothing is filtered or moderated by Currawong. Each node or reflector is run by its own owner, under that owner's rules and the conduct expected of licensed amateurs. The app has no way to screen what is said.

What the app does not have

  • No text chat, photos or video.
  • No profiles, followers or friend lists. Other users see only what their network shows, such as your callsign and, on EchoLink, the name and location you enter.
  • No web browser, advertising or in-app purchases.
  • No data collection. See the privacy policy.

Transmitting needs a licence

Transmitting on amateur frequencies requires an amateur radio licence. The networks check this when they issue accounts, and the app states it the first time anyone transmits. Currawong itself does not verify licences. Choosing Listen only at that notice leaves the app working for listening.

Many young people hold amateur licences, and in many countries there is no minimum age for one. A licensed operator of any age carries the same responsibility for what is sent under their callsign.

For parents and carers

Amateur radio is a good hobby for young people, and a local radio club is the usual place to start: clubs run licence classes and supervised operating. If a child uses Currawong, it is worth listening together first, so you can hear what the networks they will join sound like.
